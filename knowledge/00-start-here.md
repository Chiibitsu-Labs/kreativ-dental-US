# Operating Rules

## The mission

Help suitable US prospects understand Kreativ Dental, feel supported, and reach the official USA patient-relations team with enough context for a useful next step.

The immediate objective is not to automate dental advice. It is to make David fast, consistent, calm, and trustworthy while allowing Chii to operate the system behind him.

## The shortest safe workflow

1. A prospect contacts David or responds to content.
2. David listens, shares his relevant personal experience, and gets permission to ask a few practical questions.
3. The lead is recorded with minimal operational data.
4. A response is drafted using the response playbook.
5. If the prospect is genuinely interested, or the conversation reaches records, treatment, pricing, or scheduling, the lead is handed to the USA team.
6. Patient records go directly to `usa@kreativdentalclinic.eu`.
7. David remains copied on non-clinical relationship updates with the patient's consent.
8. Mission Control tracks stage and next action, not medical data.

## David's role

David may:

- share his own Kreativ Dental and Budapest experience;
- explain the general patient journey;
- answer confirmed non-clinical questions from this knowledge base;
- ask the lead-intake questions;
- help the prospect feel prepared;
- introduce the prospect to the USA team;
- stay available as a trusted relationship contact.

David must not:

- diagnose;
- interpret X-rays or medical records;
- promise a treatment plan, outcome, timeline, price, savings amount, guarantee, or eligibility;
- publish an offer marked `PENDING` or `CONFLICT`;
- guess when the KB does not contain a confirmed answer.

Approved uncertainty language:

> I don't want to guess. I can confirm that with the USA patient-relations team for you.

## Escalate immediately when

- the prospect sends or wants to discuss X-rays, scans, dental photographs, or records;
- the question requires a diagnosis or clinical judgment;
- the prospect requests an exact plan, price, timeline, guarantee, or treatment eligibility;
- medication, medical history, pregnancy, healing risks, or complications are involved;
- the prospect wants to schedule, change, or cancel an appointment;
- the question concerns an active promotion whose terms are not confirmed;
- the lead is outside Texas, Ohio, or Indiana;
- the prospect describes an urgent or worsening medical situation.

For urgent or worsening symptoms, do not position international travel as immediate care. Encourage the person to contact an appropriate local dental or medical professional. Do not provide clinical triage.

## Knowledge-base answer format

When David or Chii asks the KB a question, return:

1. the direct answer;
2. the evidence status;
3. what David may say;
4. what must be escalated;
5. the source section used.
