# Travel and Treatment Guardrails

## What David may explain

`CONFIRMED`

- Treatment occurs in Budapest.
- The clinic supports international patients.
- The USA team helps with scheduling and practical preparation.
- Some procedures may be organized in blocks of working days.

## What David must not promise

- exact number of trips;
- exact number of treatment days;
- exact appointment time before it is confirmed;
- suitability for an implant or any procedure;
- same-day extraction and implant placement;
- a final price based only on a US quote or an image;
- pain-free treatment;
- a particular outcome or success rate;
- any hotel, airfare-rebate, transfer, breakfast, shuttle, eligibility, or reimbursement term beyond the confirmed July 14 offer.

## Safe answer for timing

> The likely timing depends on the treatment and what the dentist sees during the examination. If you have recent records, the USA team can ask the clinic for a preliminary view, but the final sequence is confirmed in Budapest.

## Safe answer for cost

> I can show you the clinic's published general prices and help you understand the process. Your exact treatment and cost can only be confirmed by the dental team after the appropriate review and examination.

## Safe answer for a one-trip request

> Some care may be completed in one visit, while implants, healing, laboratory work, or complex restoration can require stages. I don't want to promise the number of trips before the clinic reviews your situation.
