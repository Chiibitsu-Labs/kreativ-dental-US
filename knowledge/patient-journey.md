# Patient Journey

This is the best-supported working journey reconstructed from the agent materials and sample USA correspondence.

## 1. Awareness

The prospect discovers Kreativ Dental through a personal referral, state website, Facebook page, community content, media mention, or helpful conversation.

## 2. First conversation

David listens, reflects the person's situation, shares only the relevant part of his own experience, and asks permission to gather practical context.

Goal: determine whether the person has genuine interest in exploring Budapest, not pressure them into a decision.

## 3. Initial qualification

Capture only the minimum operational information defined in [lead intake](../playbooks/lead-intake.md).

The person does not need to have records before being considered interested. Records become relevant when they want the clinic to comment on possible treatment, timing, or cost.

## 4. USA-team introduction

Introduce a genuinely interested prospect to Craig/Bronwyn through `usa@kreativdentalclinic.eu`.

Bring them in earlier if the conversation reaches:

- X-rays, records, or photographs;
- clinical suitability;
- theoretical treatment plan;
- detailed pricing or timing;
- booking or travel coordination.

## 5. Records and preliminary review

The patient sends available X-rays, CBCT scans, photographs, dental notes, or a local treatment plan directly to the USA inbox. The USA team forwards relevant material to the Budapest dental team.

Any remote opinion or theoretical plan is provisional.

## 6. Appointment planning

The USA team coordinates consultation timing and practical travel information. Exact treatment timing can depend on in-person findings and laboratory requirements.

## 7. Budapest examination

At the clinic, the patient completes registration, receives required diagnostic imaging, and is examined. The dental team then confirms the treatment plan, sequence, timing, and cost before treatment obligations are accepted.

## 8. Treatment and possible staged visits

Some treatment can require healing periods and return visits. Agents must not promise that all work will be completed in one trip.

## 9. Follow-up and relationship

The USA team and clinic coordinate treatment-related follow-up. David remains available as the person's relationship contact without becoming the clinical decision-maker.

## Stage ownership

| Stage | Primary owner | David's role |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness and first contact | David / Chii | Lead relationship |
| Qualification | David / Chii | Listen and gather context |
| Handoff | David / Chii | Introduce and remain copied with consent |
| Records and clinical review | USA team / Budapest team | Support, do not interpret |
| Scheduling and travel coordination | USA team | Reassure and stay available |
| Treatment | Budapest team | Relationship support only |
| Post-treatment relationship | USA team + David | Follow up within approved boundaries |
