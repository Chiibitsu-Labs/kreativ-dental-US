# Claims Register

Public marketing must use evidence, current wording, and appropriate conditions. Inclusion in an agent manual is not automatic approval for publication.

## Safe positioning with ordinary review

| Claim | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kreativ Dental is in Budapest, Hungary | Approved | Basic fact |
| The clinic serves international patients | Approved | Repeated official positioning |
| USA-based patient-relations support is available | Approved | Current USA contact materials |
| Final treatment decisions require clinical examination | Required | Include whenever discussing preliminary plans |
| David can share his own experience | Approved with accuracy | First-person statements only; do not generalize his result |
| Current USA patient offer | Approved with conditions | Three partner-hotel nights plus USD $350 airfare rebate; state the USD $3,000 minimum, treatment-completion requirement, availability, and booking conditions |
| Complimentary consultation and diagnostic X-ray | Approved as separate support | Do not describe these as part of the three-night/airfare promotion |

## Requires substantiation and approval before public use

| Claim area | Current status | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| "Up to 70%" or "50-75%" savings | Pending | Current comparable-price methodology, typicality, conditions, and approved disclaimer |
| 28, 30, or another number of years operating | Conflict | Confirm founding/operating date and approved wording |
| Award-winning / world-renowned / world-class | Pending | Award names, issuer, year, scope, and current approved wording |
| ISO/accreditation/membership claims | Pending | Current certificates or official registry evidence |
| Hungary/Budapest safety rankings | Pending | Current primary ranking source and careful contextual wording |
| 1.2 million Americans travelling to Mexico for dental care | Pending | Reliable current source and exact definition/year |
| Implant or procedure success rates | Pending | Clinic-specific evidence, population, period, and clinical/legal approval |
| Guarantees and warranty terms | Pending | Current written policy and exclusions |
| Tax deductibility | Pending | Current qualified US tax/legal review; never individualized advice |
| Only clinic with an airport kiosk or similar exclusivity | Pending | Verifiable current evidence |

## Prohibited

- diagnosing or promising a treatment plan before examination;
- guaranteeing an outcome, price, savings amount, or treatment duration;
- describing a competitor as corrupt, unsafe, inferior, or dishonest without approved, legally reviewed substantiation;
- implying that every patient qualifies for an offer;
- implying that David or Chii is a dentist or clinician;
- fabricated scarcity, testimonials, or review content;
- using a patient's story, image, records, or outcome without documented permission.

## Approval record format

For an approved claim, record:

```text
Claim:
Exact approved wording:
Evidence link/file:
Evidence date:
Approved by:
Approval date:
Conditions/disclaimer:
Review date:
```
