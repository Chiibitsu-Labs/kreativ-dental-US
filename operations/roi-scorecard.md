# Weekly ROI Scorecard

The scorecard shows David what changed because Chii operated the system. Report leading indicators immediately and revenue indicators when they become available.

## Delivery

- KB pages added or updated;
- unresolved conflicts converted into owner questions;
- David questions answered from the KB;
- response drafts produced;
- content pieces prepared, approved, and published;
- automations/workflows shipped.

## Demand

- new inquiries;
- inquiry source by state;
- meaningful conversations;
- qualified leads;
- handoffs;
- out-of-state referrals;
- average first-response time;
- follow-ups completed on time.

## Progression

- handoffs acknowledged;
- records requested;
- clinic reviews;
- consultations offered;
- appointments booked;
- travel confirmed;
- treatment starts;
- completions reported;
- closed-lost count and high-level reason.

## Commercial

- leads with acknowledged David attribution;
- treatment/commission pipeline where supported;
- commission earned;
- commission paid;
- disputed or unknown attribution.

Do not estimate commission as earned without an agreed basis and authoritative status.

## Efficiency

- estimated hours of David administration avoided;
- estimated hours of Chii drafting/research saved by reusable systems;
- percentage of routine questions answered without escalation;
- handoff acknowledgement time;
- overdue next actions.

## Weekly narrative

Use five lines:

1. What shipped.
2. What generated conversations.
3. What moved closer to a booking.
4. What is blocked by the clinic/marketing team.
5. What will be done next week.
