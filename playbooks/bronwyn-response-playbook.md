# Bronwyn-Style Response Playbook

This playbook is distilled from supplied examples of successful USA patient correspondence. It captures the method, not patient-identifying content.

## What makes the responses work

1. Warm human introduction.
2. Specific reflection of the person's situation.
3. Calm reassurance without promising an outcome.
4. Direct answers to every practical question that can safely be answered.
5. Honest boundary around what requires the dental team.
6. One clear next step.
7. Practical reassurance about Budapest and the process when relevant.
8. Personal availability without pressure.

## Drafting method

Copy the prospect's message into a private drafting area temporarily. Work through it point by point, borrowing their natural vocabulary where appropriate. Delete the copied message before saving the final reusable draft.

Do not place a raw patient message in this repository or an AI project that has not been approved for patient data.

## Response structure

### 1. Open warmly

> Thank you for reaching out. I'm David, the Kreativ Dental representative for Texas, Ohio, and Indiana, and I have personally been through the Budapest experience.

Adjust the territory sentence for out-of-state leads; do not imply territorial ownership.

### 2. Reflect the situation

> Receiving a quote like that can feel overwhelming, especially when you are already trying to manage discomfort and work out what is actually necessary.

Use only facts the person provided.

### 3. Give the safe direct answer

Answer confirmed general questions from the KB. Do not pad the message with unrelated selling points.

### 4. State the boundary

> The dental team would need to review the appropriate records and examine you before confirming treatment, timing, or cost, so I don't want to guess.

### 5. Ask for the smallest next step

> If you would like, I can introduce you to the USA patient-relations team. If you have recent X-rays or a local treatment plan, you can send them directly to the official USA inbox in that email thread.

### 6. Close personally

> I am happy to stay copied and help you understand the process as you decide whether it is right for you.

## Tone

- calm;
- warm;
- respectful;
- plainspoken;
- specific;
- hopeful without hype;
- never shaming about dental condition or finances.

## Avoid

- hard-closing language;
- fake urgency;
- diagnosing from a description or photograph;
- claiming the person will save a particular amount;
- saying a procedure will work;
- implying Budapest is the only sensible choice;
- attacking Mexico, Turkey, another clinic, or a local dentist;
- publishing an unconfirmed promotion;
- long generic paragraphs that ignore the person's actual question.

## Quality check

Before sending, confirm:

- every question was answered or explicitly routed;
- no clinical claim or promise slipped in;
- the next step is obvious;
- no unconfirmed offer or public claim appears;
- the message sounds like a person, not a brochure;
- sensitive attachments are routed to the official USA inbox.
