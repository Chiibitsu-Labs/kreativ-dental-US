# 4+1 Content Playbook

`WORKING_RULE`

The internal cadence is five publishing days: four useful, human, or enjoyable posts and one promotional post. The US state market may adapt topics, format, and sequence based on performance.

## Purpose

Build familiarity and trust before asking for action. Content should help anxious people feel less alone, make Budapest feel understandable, and give David a credible reason to start conversations.

## Weekly pattern

### Day 1 - Human / Budapest

Show the city, food, culture, travel practicality, or a personal moment. Avoid unsupported safety rankings.

### Day 2 - Anxiety / empathy

Address avoidance, embarrassment, fear, or overwhelm calmly. Never shame damaged teeth or use graphic images casually.

### Day 3 - Process / FAQ

Explain one step: records, provisional plans, working days, staged treatment, travel preparation, or what an agent can and cannot answer.

### Day 4 - David / community

Use David's first-person experience, a behind-the-scenes update, a local connection, or a state-relevant community angle.

### Day 5 - Promotion / invitation

Invite people with significant dental quotes or treatment needs to ask questions. Use only an approved current offer. If the offer is still unresolved, promote the conversation rather than a discount.

Safe CTA:

> Facing a large dental quote and wondering whether Budapest could be an option? Message David for a no-pressure conversation about how the process works.

## State adaptation

- Ohio and Indiana can share much of the same base content with local references adjusted.
- Texas should have its own positioning and comparisons; never attack cross-border care or make unsupported safety claims.
- Use state-specific landing-page and Facebook links once Peter confirms them.

## Content approval check

Before scheduling:

- claim appears in the claims register as approved;
- offer is current and conditions are included;
- no clinical promise;
- no patient image, story, or testimonial without documented permission;
- no patient data;
- CTA routes to David's correct state asset or contact;
- tone is helpful, not spammy.

## What to measure

- posts published;
- saves and meaningful comments;
- direct inquiries;
- qualified conversations;
- handoffs;
- appointments/bookings attributable to content;
- top-performing topic by state.
