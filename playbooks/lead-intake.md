# Lead Intake Playbook

## Purpose

Collect enough context to decide the next helpful step without interrogating the person or collecting unnecessary medical information.

## Qualification principle

A qualified lead is not someone David has medically assessed. It is someone who:

- appears to need or be considering significant dental treatment;
- is open to learning about treatment in Budapest;
- wants a concrete next step;
- agrees to be introduced to the USA team when appropriate.

## Minimum questions

Ask conversationally, not as a form dump.

1. What is happening with your teeth or dental quote right now?
2. What would you most like to be able to change or achieve?
3. Have you been given a treatment plan or approximate quote locally?
4. Would you consider travelling to Budapest if the option made sense?
5. Is there a particular time you would ideally want to travel?
6. Do you have recent X-rays, scans, photographs, or dental notes that you could send directly to the USA patient-relations team?
7. May I introduce you to the USA team and remain copied so I can continue helping?

Do not require every answer before being helpful.

## Minimal operational record

Mission Control may store:

- generated lead ID;
- name and preferred contact details;
- state;
- source/channel;
- high-level need category;
- local quote band, if volunteered;
- desired outcome in the prospect's own words;
- travel willingness and rough timing;
- whether records are available;
- consent to introduce/share contact information;
- stage, owner, next action, and dates;
- attribution fields.

Do not store:

- X-rays, scans, clinical photographs, dental records, or attachments;
- detailed diagnosis or treatment history;
- medications, health conditions, or medical history;
- passport, payment-card, banking, or insurance documents;
- unnecessary family or personal details.

## Lead priority

### High

- explicitly wants the next step;
- has a significant treatment need or large quote;
- is open to Budapest;
- has records or is willing to obtain/send them;
- has a plausible travel window.

### Medium

- relevant need and curiosity;
- not yet ready to share records, travel, or commit to a next conversation;
- needs education or reassurance.

### Nurture

- no current need or timeline;
- only browsing;
- not currently open to travel.

### Escalate without qualification

- clinical or urgent-health question;
- records already sent;
- request to book;
- current offer or guarantee question;
- out-of-state lead needing territory routing.
